Utkal Eventz: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

1. Who we are

Utkal Eventz ("we", "us") provides DJ, sound, light, tent and event services, high-tech speaker cabinets and an electrical and music shop in Jagatpur, Cuttack, Odisha, India. This policy explains what information the Utkal Eventz mobile app collects and how we use it. Contact: info@utkaleventz.com, +91 63614 72511.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account. We collect information only when you choose to send an enquiry: your name, mobile number, event type, event date (optional), the services or items you select and any message you type. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, and the app does not accept payments or card details. Like most apps, the services we use (see section 4) may process basic technical data such as IP address and device identifiers to run and secure the service.

3. How we use your information

We use your enquiry details only to contact you, prepare a quotation, check availability, arrange and deliver the service you asked about, and to improve our service. We do not sell your personal information and we do not show ads in the app.

4. Where your information is stored and who can access it

Enquiries you submit are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), a cloud service that processes data on our behalf. Only Utkal Eventz can read these enquiries. If you choose to send an enquiry through WhatsApp, email or phone call, that message or call is handled by the app you pick and is subject to its own privacy policy. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and complete the service, and normally delete them within 12 months after the enquiry or event.

6. Your choices and deletion

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information at any time by writing to info@utkaleventz.com or calling +91 63614 72511 with the name and mobile number you used. We will act on verified requests within a reasonable time.

7. Security

Data is sent to our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access is restricted. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The app is meant for adults who book events and buy equipment. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available in the app and on our website, with the date it was last updated.

10. Contact us
